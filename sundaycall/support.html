Questions people ask

What is it for?

Sunday Call is a notebook for a busy parent. You add your kids (and, if you like, family, grandchildren and friends), and one glance tells you where things stand with each of them: who reached out and is waiting to hear back, who you haven't talked to in a while and what's coming up in their lives that is worth asking about.

It is yours alone. Your family doesn't install anything, and the app never contacts them or tells them anything.

Who is the family I see when I first open it?

An example family, so you can see how it works before you set anything up. Tap Set up mine on the banner at the top of Today, and your own family takes its place.

What do the dot and the dashed ring mean?

  • A dot means they reached out: a text or a missed call you haven't answered. It clears when you call or text back, once you've talked, or when you tap the tick to say you've answered.
  • A dashed ring means it has been longer than usual since you talked or reached out. "Usually" is the rhythm you chose for that person, and you can pick "No set rhythm" if you'd rather not have one.
  • A plain ring means nothing needs you.

The At a glance tab has the same key with pictures.

Why doesn't it know my daughter texted me?

No app can see your calls and texts. iPhone keeps them private, which is as it should be. Calls and texts you start from Sunday Call are recorded for you. Anything else you log with the Log button, which takes a couple of taps, or with the tick once you've answered.

How does a call get its length?

When you call from Sunday Call, iPhone tells the app when the call connected and when it ended, but never who it was with. If iPhone doesn't pass that along in time, the app asks you roughly how long it was. If no one picked up, choose No answer: it counts as reaching out, not as talking.

How do I put it on my Lock Screen or Home Screen?

The At a glance tab walks you through both, step by step. On the Lock Screen you can show up to four people as rings, or short lists. On the Home Screen, the small widget shows whoever needs you most and the wide one shows four people.

Can people see names on my Lock Screen?

Not unless you want them to. The Lock Screen shows initials until you turn on Full names on the Lock Screen in At a glance. Your notes never appear there.

What is the tick on the widget?

When someone is waiting to hear back, the small Home Screen widget shows a tick next to their name. Once you've answered them (in Messages, say), tap the tick and the dot clears without opening the app.

Can I log a call with Siri?

Yes. Say "I talked to someone in Sunday Call" and Siri asks who. Your phone has to be unlocked, so no one else can ask it for your list of people.

Will it send me reminders?

Only if you ask it to. There are two kinds, both off to start with: one the day after something you meant to ask about, at a time you choose, and a short summary on Sunday morning. There are never more than three in a week, and a reminder never shows what you wrote. Turn them on from the gear at the top of Today.

One of my kids is going through something, or wants some space.

Open their page, tap Edit and choose under Right now: Giving them space, Going through something or Home with me (until a date you pick). Each changes what Sunday Call flags for them. Nothing is deleted.

Someone has died. What should I do with them?

I'm sorry. Choose A memory under Right now. They move off Today to a quiet page of their own, nothing about them is flagged or counted, and everything you wrote is kept. Removing them instead deletes it all.

How do I add people or change settings later?

Tap the gear at the top of Today. It opens the setup walkthrough again, where you can add people, show or hide grandchildren and friends, and change reminders.

Is there a limit on how many people?

Up to six kids, two family members, ten grandchildren and eight friends. It is meant for the people you keep up with closely, not your whole address book.

Where is everything kept? What about a new phone?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no cloud copy of our own. If you move to a new iPhone by restoring from a backup (iCloud or your computer), Sunday Call comes across with everything else. Deleting the app deletes what's in it.

What does it need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Calling and texting from the app need a phone plan, the same as the Phone and Messages apps.

Purchases and refunds
