Questions people ask

How do we play?

Lay the iPhone flat on the table between you. Orange sits at the bottom of the phone and Blue sits at the top, and everything Blue needs to read is turned to face them. Pick a game on the first screen. Each game shows you what to do the first time.

What are the four games?

  • Slide: take turns sliding discs onto the rings. The disc nearest the middle scores. Drag toward the rings and let go.
  • Snap: cards are dealt in the middle. When two in a row match, tap your button first.
  • Boxes: take turns drawing a line between two dots. Close the fourth side of a box and it is yours, and you go again.
  • Pairs: turn two cards. If they match, they are yours and you go again.

What does "Help for" do?

It evens things up when one player is much younger, or much older, than the other. Choose who gets help and how much. The other player can see what the help is doing, so nobody is fooled.

  • Slide: with a lot of help, they shoot last unless they are ahead. With a little, they shoot last when they are behind.
  • Snap: the other player's tap counts a moment late, a little longer with a lot of help. The other player's button says Tapped while it waits.
  • Boxes: they draw first and are shown which lines close a box and which give one away. When a box is left to take, the other player stops after three boxes in a turn, or two with a lot of help.
  • Pairs: after a miss they can turn one more card, once a game with a little help or three times with a lot.

How do we leave a game?

Hold the cross in the corner for a second. A quick tap does nothing, so an elbow on the table won't end a game.

Can we play on two phones?

Not in this version. Everything is played on one iPhone between you.

There's no sound.

Sounds follow the silent switch on the side of the iPhone, and turn off with the Sound button on the first screen. They play alongside music or a podcast rather than stopping it.

Does it work with VoiceOver and larger text?

The first screen and the buttons work with VoiceOver, and the game board takes touches directly while VoiceOver is on. Snap and Slide depend on seeing the table, so they are best played with someone who can. The games respect Reduce Motion.

What does it need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It works with no internet connection.
