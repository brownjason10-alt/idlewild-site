Privacy Policy

Idlewild Shore · last updated 27 August 2026

Idlewild Shore collects no data about you. It has no account, no sign-in, and it makes no network connections of any kind. Nothing you do in it leaves your iPhone.

What the app stores

One file, on your iPhone, holding the shells you have picked up so the drawer still has them next time. It records the shells and nothing else — no identifier, no dates, no session history, no count.

That file lives inside the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it. Like other app data it may be included in an iPhone backup you make yourself, to your own computer or your own iCloud account; that copy is yours and is not visible to me.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage reporting.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with anyone for any purpose.
  • No third-party software development kits of any kind.
  • No account, no email address, no name, no sign-in.
  • No location permission. The sky follows the real sun, worked out on the phone from your time zone setting alone — that is never sent anywhere.
  • No access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, calendar, health data, or files.
  • No push notifications.

Purchases

Idlewild Shore is bought once, from the App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details, and the app itself stores nothing about the transaction.

What Apple may show me

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics and crash reports with app developers, Apple may show me aggregate, anonymised statistics — how many people downloaded the app, and crash reports with no identity attached. That is Apple's mechanism, it is off unless you turned it on, and the app plays no part in it. It does not identify you.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, which includes children. There is nothing in it that could identify a child, and nothing to delete on request beyond deleting the app.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data — under Canadian law, the GDPR, or anywhere else — apply to personal data that has been collected. None has been. There is no copy of anything about you to send you or to delete, and deleting the app removes the only file the app ever wrote.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever does something this page does not describe, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
