Idlewild Shore

A lake to watch while you wait.

Questions people ask

How do I stop?

Touch the top-left corner of the screen at any moment. Anything you picked up that session is already kept — there is nothing to lose by leaving early, and nothing that has to be finished.

Where do the shells go?

Into the drawer, in the colours they had on the beach. It keeps what you have picked up across every session, and it lives only on your iPhone. It is not counted and there is no total anywhere.

There is no sound.

The app follows the ring/silent switch on the side of the phone, the same way a video in a message does. If the switch is set to silent, the lake is silent. Flip it and the sound comes back.

It is meant to be watchable with the sound off — a waiting room is rarely a quiet place.

Why is the light different every time?

The sky follows the real sun. The app works out sunrise and sunset for your time zone, on the phone, from the time zone alone — so an early morning is not the same session as a late evening, and a December afternoon is not a June one.

It never asks for your location, and nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

Can I choose a different time of day?

There is a quiet line under Begin that opens the other one.

How long is it?

Seven minutes. You can leave at any second.

What is the water doing?

The waves start at about the speed of an ordinary breath and get slower across the seven minutes. If your breathing goes along with them, it slows too. Nothing asks you to do that and nothing checks whether you did — it is just what the water is doing, said plainly here because it seems better to say it than to leave it as a surprise.

It is a place to look at. It is not a medical or health product, it makes no health claims, and it is not a substitute for care from a professional.

VoiceOver

The two pace marks are reachable with VoiceOver, in the same places the touch controls are. If something on the shore is not reachable and should be, that is worth an email — it is the kind of thing that is easy to miss and easy to fix.

What does it need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Purchases and refunds

Idlewild Shore is bought once. There is no subscription, nothing to renew, and nothing else to buy inside it.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple rather than by me — the place to ask is reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account. The whole policy is on the privacy page, and it is short.
