Paint Shed

Three quiet puzzles about colour.

Questions people ask

What are the three rooms?

  • Stamp Order (20 levels): make your paper match the small picture at the top. Each stamp covers whatever is under it, so the one you can see whole went down last. Work backwards from it.
  • Mixing Pot (24 levels): add drops of red, yellow and blue (later white and black) until the pot matches a part of the picture, then tap that part to paint it. The words under the pot say how close you are and which way to go.
  • Three Colour Map (30 levels): colour every piece of land with three colours so that no two touching pieces match. Ringed pieces are clues and can't change. Water stays blank.

The first level of each room walks you through it, and the ? at the top of any level shows the room's how-to again.

I'm stuck.

Press Hint. It always shows the next step, and in Three Colour Map it says why. Hints lower your stars for that level, and nothing else.

How do stars work?

Three for a clean solve. In Stamp Order you lose them for wrong tries, in Mixing Pot for drops over the fewest the level needs, and in every room for hints. Your best result on each level is kept, so a level can be played again for more.

Why can't I open the next level?

Levels open one at a time, by solving the one before. Each room keeps its own place, and the room's card on the first screen takes you back to it.

How do I start again?

One level: Start over (Stamp Order and Mixing Pot) or Clear (Three Colour Map). Everything: Settings, then Erase progress. Hold the button, or tap it and confirm. That clears every solved level and star, and the how-to cards will show again. It can't be undone.

There's no sound.

Sounds follow the silent switch on the side of the iPhone, and turn off with Sound in Settings. They play alongside music or a podcast rather than stopping it.

Does it work with VoiceOver and larger text?

It is made to. Every stamp, part of a picture and piece of land is its own VoiceOver item, with its name and state read out, and the buttons are labelled. Text follows your iPhone's text size; inside a level it stops growing at a large size so the board stays playable. With Reduce Motion on, the pulsing hints hold still and the paint drops on the win card are left out. The app follows your iPhone's light or dark appearance.

What does it need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It works with no internet connection.
